# **How to Treat and Manage Dry Mouth**

Treatment for dry mouth depends on what is causing it.

If a medicine is causing your dry mouth, your health care provider or dentist may:

- Change the amount of medicine you take.
- Switch you to a different medicine.
- Recommend artificial saliva to keep your mouth moist.
- Prescribe medicine to help your salivary glands make more saliva.

### **What Can You Do to Relieve Dry Mouth?**

There are several things you can do to help your mouth feel better:

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
- Avoid drinks with caffeine, such as coffee, tea, and some sodas, because they can make your mouth drier.
- Chew sugar-free gum or suck on sugar-free hard candy to help your mouth make more saliva.
- Avoid spicy or salty foods because they may hurt your dry mouth.
- Avoid alcohol and tobacco.
- Use a humidifier at night to add moisture to the air.
- Use mouthwash made especially for dry mouth.

### **How Can You Protect Your Teeth?**

Having a dry mouth can increase your risk of tooth decay and cavities.

To protect your teeth:

- Brush your teeth regularly.
- Floss every day.
- Visit your dentist twice a year.

### **Remember**

Drinking water, avoiding certain foods and drinks, and keeping your mouth clean can help relieve dry mouth.

Talk with your health care provider or dentist if your dry mouth does not improve. They can help find the cause and recommend treatment.

*Source: NIH – National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research*
