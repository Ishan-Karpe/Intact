# **Understanding Cryptosporidiosis**

Cryptosporidiosis is an illness caused by a tiny parasite called *Cryptosporidium*. It can affect your stomach and intestines.

### **What Are the Symptoms?**

The most common symptom is watery diarrhea.

Other symptoms may include:

- Dehydration (losing too much water from your body).
- Weight loss.
- Stomach cramps or pain.
- Fever.
- Nausea (feeling sick to your stomach).
- Vomiting (throwing up).

Some people may have the infection but never have any symptoms.

### **When Do Symptoms Start and How Long Do They Last?**

Symptoms usually begin **after getting infected**.

For people with healthy immune systems:

- Symptoms usually last about 1 to 2 weeks.
- Sometimes, symptoms last longer or come and go for up to 30 days.

### **Who Is at Risk for More Serious Illness?**

People with weak immune systems may become very sick. They may have symptoms that last a long time.

Your immune system helps your body fight infections. Your immune system may be weak if you:

- Have HIV.
- Have cancer.
- Have a genetic condition that affects your immune system.
- Take certain medicines, such as cancer treatment medicines (chemotherapy) or medicines used after an organ transplant.

### **When Should You See a Health Care Provider?**

Contact your health care provider if:

- You have watery diarrhea that lasts more than a few days.
- You have a weak immune system and develop diarrhea or other symptoms.

**Remember:** Cryptosporidiosis can cause diarrhea and other stomach problems. It is especially important to get medical help if you have a weak immune system.
