# **Understanding Your Cholesterol Numbers**

Cholesterol is a type of fat found in your blood. Your body needs some cholesterol, but having too much can increase your risk of heart disease.

Cholesterol levels are measured in **milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL)**.

Your healthy cholesterol levels may depend on your age, race, blood pressure, weight, family health history, and other factors. Talk with your health care provider about what your numbers should be.

The following numbers are general guidelines for healthy cholesterol levels.

### **Healthy Cholesterol Levels for Children and Teens (Age 19 and Younger)**

| Type of cholesterol    | Healthy level       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| Total cholesterol      | More than 170 mg/dL |
| Non-HDL cholesterol    | Less than 120 mg/dL |
| LDL (bad cholesterol)  | |
| HDL (good cholesterol) | More than 45 mg/dL  |

### **Healthy Cholesterol Levels for Men (Age 20 and Older)**

| Type of cholesterol    | Healthy level              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Total cholesterol      | Less than 200 mg/dL        |
| Non-HDL cholesterol    | Less than 130 mg/dL        |
| LDL (bad cholesterol)  | Less than 100 mg/dL        |
| HDL (good cholesterol) | 60 mg/dL or higher is best |

For men, an HDL level more than 40 mg/dL is considered low.

### **Healthy Cholesterol Levels for Women (Age 20 and Older)**

| Type of cholesterol    | Healthy level              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Total cholesterol      | Less than 200 mg/dL        |
| Non-HDL cholesterol    | Less than 130 mg/dL        |
| LDL (bad cholesterol)  | Less than 100 mg/dL        |
| HDL (good cholesterol) | 60 mg/dL or higher is best |

For women, an HDL level below 50 mg/dL is considered low.

### **What Do the Different Types of Cholesterol Mean?**

- **Total cholesterol:** The total amount of cholesterol in your blood.
- **LDL (bad cholesterol):** Too much LDL can build up in your blood vessels and increase your risk of heart disease.
- **HDL (good cholesterol):** Helps remove extra cholesterol from your blood.
- **Non-HDL cholesterol:** Includes all types of cholesterol except HDL. Lower levels are generally healthier.

### **What Are Triglycerides?**

Triglycerides are a type of fat in your blood. They are not cholesterol, but they are measured during the same blood test.

Healthy triglyceride levels are:

| Triglyceride level  | What it means   |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| Below 300 mg/dL     | Normal          |
| 150–199 mg/dL       | Borderline high |
| | High            |

If your triglyceride levels are borderline high or high, your health care provider may recommend treatment.

### **Remember**

Your cholesterol numbers help your health care provider understand your risk of heart disease. Even if your numbers are within the general healthy range, your provider can help you understand what is best for your health.

Talk with your health care provider about your cholesterol test results and whether you need to make changes to your diet, exercise habits, or take medicine.
