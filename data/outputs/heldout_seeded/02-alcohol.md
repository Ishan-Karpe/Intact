# **Understanding Excessive Drinking**

Excessive drinking means drinking too much alcohol. It includes **binge drinking** and **heavy drinking**.

### **What is binge drinking?**

Binge drinking means drinking a large amount of alcohol in a short time. This can raise your blood alcohol level to 0.08% or higher.

- **For men:** Having 5 or more drinks within a few hours.
- **For women:** Having 4 or more drinks within a few hours.

### **What is heavy drinking?**

Heavy drinking means drinking more than the following amounts:

- **For men:** More than 5 drinks in one day or more than 15 drinks in one week.
- **For women:** More than 4 drinks in one day or more than 8 drinks in one week.

### **How can excessive drinking affect your health?**

Binge drinking can increase your chances of:

- Getting hurt or having an accident.
- Being in a car crash.
- Alcohol poisoning (drinking so much that it becomes dangerous).
- Becoming violent or being hurt by someone else.

Drinking too much over a long period can lead to serious health problems, including:

- Alcohol use disorder (difficulty controlling how much you drink).
- Liver damage and liver disease.
- Heart problems.
- A higher risk of certain types of cancer.
- A higher risk of injuries.

### **How can heavy drinking affect your life?**

Drinking too much can also cause problems with:

- Family and home life.
- Work.
- Friendships and relationships.

### **Help is available**

If you or someone you know drinks too much, help is available. Treatment can help people reduce or stop drinking and improve their health and daily lives.

*Source: NIH – National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism*
