# Understanding Female Infertility

Infertility means being unable to get pregnant after trying for at least one year. For women over age 35, infertility means being unable to get pregnant after 6 months of trying. Having repeated miscarriages can also be considered infertility.

Female infertility can be caused by several things, including age, physical problems, hormone changes, and lifestyle or environmental factors.

## Common Causes of Female Infertility

Most cases of female infertility are related to problems with producing or releasing eggs.

Two conditions that can cause these problems are:

- **Primary ovarian insufficiency:** When a woman's ovaries stop working properly before the age of natural menopause.
- **Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS):** A condition that can prevent the ovaries from releasing eggs regularly or from releasing healthy eggs.

## What Causes Infertility?

Infertility does not always come from the woman. About one-third of cases are caused by problems in the woman, and another third are caused by problems in the man. In some cases, doctors cannot find a clear cause.

## Getting Help and Treatment

If you think you may have fertility problems, talk to your doctor. Tests can help find out whether you have infertility and what may be causing it.

If a cause is found, treatment may include:

- Medicines
- Surgery
- Assisted reproductive technologies (medical treatments that help people become pregnant)

Fortunately, many couples who receive treatment for infertility are able to have children.

*Source: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office on Women's Health*
