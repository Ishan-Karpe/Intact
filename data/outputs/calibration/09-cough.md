# Understanding Coughs

Coughing is a natural reaction that helps keep your throat and breathing passages clear. Even though coughing can be annoying, it helps protect your body and get rid of things that may cause irritation.

There are two main types of coughs:

- **Acute coughs:** These start suddenly and usually last no longer than 2 to 3 weeks. They often happen with a cold, the flu, or short-term bronchitis.
- **Chronic coughs:** These last longer than 2 to 3 weeks. They may be caused by several conditions, including:
  - Long-term bronchitis
  - Asthma
  - Allergies
  - COPD (a lung disease that makes breathing difficult)
  - GERD (a condition where stomach acid flows back up into the throat)
  - Smoking
  - Throat problems, such as croup in young children
  - Certain medicines

## Ways to Ease a Cough

Drinking water can help soothe a cough. Adding moisture to the air may also help. You can do this by taking a steamy shower or using a vaporizer.

If you have a cold or the flu, antihistamines (medicines that help with allergy symptoms) may work better than over-the-counter cough medicines.

**Cough medicine safety for children:**
- Children under 4 years old should not be given cough medicine.
- For children over 4, be careful and read the medicine label before giving it to them.
