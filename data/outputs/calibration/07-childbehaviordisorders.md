# Understanding Behavior Disorders in Children

All children misbehave sometimes. Some children may also have temporary behavior problems when they are dealing with stressful events, such as the birth of a new sibling, a divorce, or the death of a family member.

Behavior disorders are more serious. They involve a pattern of aggressive, harmful, or disruptive behavior that lasts for more than 6 months. This behavior is also not normal for the child's age.

## Warning Signs of Behavior Problems

Some warning signs include:

- Hurting or threatening to hurt themselves, other people, or pets
- Damaging or destroying things
- Lying or stealing
- Having trouble in school or skipping school
- Starting to smoke, drink alcohol, or use drugs at an early age
- Becoming sexually active at an early age
- Having frequent temper tantrums or arguments
- Often showing disrespect or hostility toward parents, teachers, or other authority figures

## Getting Help

If you notice these warning signs, ask for help. Poor behavior can turn into habits if it is not addressed.

Children with behavior problems have a higher risk of struggling in school, developing mental health problems, and even dying by suicide.

There are several ways to help. Parenting classes or family therapy can teach parents how to set clear rules and make sure children follow them. Talk therapy and behavior therapy can also help children improve their behavior.
