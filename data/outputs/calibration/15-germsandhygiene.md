# How to Protect Yourself and Others from Germs

You can help prevent the spread of germs and protect yourself and others by following these steps:

## Cover Your Coughs and Sneezes
- Cover your mouth and nose with a tissue when you cough or sneeze.
- If you do not have a tissue, use the inside of your elbow.

## Wash Your Hands Often
Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. It is especially important to wash your hands at these times:

- Before, during, and after preparing food
- Before eating
- Before and after caring for someone who is sick with vomiting or diarrhea
- Before and after treating a cut or wound
- After using the toilet
- After changing diapers or helping a child use the toilet
- After blowing your nose, coughing, or sneezing
- After touching animals, animal food, or animal waste
- After handling pet food or treats
- After touching garbage

If soap and water are not available, use a hand sanitizer that contains at least 60% alcohol.

## Other Ways to Prevent the Spread of Germs
- Stay home when you are sick.
- Avoid close contact with people who are sick.
- Handle, cook, and store food safely.
- Regularly clean and disinfect surfaces and objects that people touch often, such as doorknobs, phones, and countertops.
