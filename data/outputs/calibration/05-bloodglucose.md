# Checking Your Blood Sugar

If you have diabetes, you will probably need to check your blood sugar every day. This helps you make sure your blood sugar stays within your target range. Some people may need to check it several times a day. Ask your health care team how often you should check your blood sugar.

## Ways to Check Your Blood Sugar at Home

**Blood glucose meter**

The most common way to check your blood sugar at home is with a blood glucose meter. This device measures the amount of sugar in a small drop of blood, usually taken from your fingertip.

**Continuous glucose monitor (CGM)**

Another way to check your blood sugar is with a continuous glucose monitor (CGM). Most CGM devices use a tiny sensor placed under your skin. The sensor checks your blood sugar every few minutes.

A CGM shows how your blood sugar changes throughout the day and night. It can be especially helpful for people who take insulin and have problems with low blood sugar.

## A1C Blood Test

Your doctor will also check your blood sugar with a blood test called an A1C test. This test measures your average blood sugar level over the past 3 months.

Most people with diabetes need an A1C test at least twice a year. However, you may need it more often if your blood sugar is not meeting your treatment goals.
