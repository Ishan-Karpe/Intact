# Understanding Your A1C Test

An A1C test is a blood test that helps doctors check for type 2 diabetes and prediabetes. It shows your average blood sugar level over the past 3 months.

Doctors may use the A1C test by itself or with other tests to find out if you have diabetes. They also use it to check how well you are controlling your blood sugar if you already have diabetes.

The A1C test is different from the daily blood sugar checks that people with diabetes may do at home.

## Understanding Your Results

Your A1C result is shown as a percentage. A higher percentage means your blood sugar has been higher over the past 3 months.

- **Normal:** Below 5.7%
- **Prediabetes:** 5.7% to 6.4%. This means your blood sugar is higher than normal and you have a greater risk of developing type 2 diabetes. Your doctor may recommend getting tested again every year.
- **Diabetes:** 6.5% or higher. Your doctor may use another test to confirm the diagnosis.

## If You Have Diabetes

You should have an A1C test at least twice a year.

For many people with diabetes, the A1C goal is below 7%. However, your personal goal may be different. Ask your doctor what goal is right for you.

If your A1C result is too high, you may need to make changes to your diabetes care plan.

*Source: NIH — National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases*
