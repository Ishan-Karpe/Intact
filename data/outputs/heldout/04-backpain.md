# **Understanding Back Pain**

Back pain is a very common problem. About 8 out of 10 people experience back pain at some point in their lives.

Back pain can feel like a dull, constant ache or a sudden, sharp pain.

### **Types of Back Pain**

There are two main types of back pain:

- **Acute back pain:** Starts suddenly and usually lasts a few days to a few weeks.
- **Chronic back pain:** Lasts longer than three months.

### **What Can Help Back Pain?**

Most back pain gets better on its own, but it may take some time.

You can try:

- Taking over-the-counter pain medicine.
- Resting for a short time.
- Using hot or cold packs.

Avoid staying in bed for more than 1 or 2 days because too much bed rest can make your back pain worse.

### **When Should You See a Health Care Provider?**

Contact your health care provider if:

- Your back pain is very bad.
- Your pain does not get better after three days.
- Your back pain starts after an injury.

### **How Is Back Pain Treated?**

Treatment depends on the type of back pain you have and what is causing it.

Treatment may include:

- Hot or cold packs.
- Exercise and physical activity.
- Medicines.
- Injections to help relieve pain.
- Other treatments, such as massage or acupuncture.
- Surgery in some cases.

### **Remember**

Back pain is common, and most people get better with time. If your pain is severe, does not improve, or happens after an injury, contact your health care provider.

*Source: NIH – National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases*
